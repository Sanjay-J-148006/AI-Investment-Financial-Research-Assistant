NVIDIA CORPORATION — ANNUAL FINANCIAL & STRATEGIC SUMMARY (FISCAL YEAR 2024)

1. BUSINESS OVERVIEW
NVIDIA Corporation is the global leader in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence hardware and software platforms.
The company operates through two primary segments: Compute & Networking (Data Center GPUs, Networking, Quantum Computing platforms) and Graphics (GeForce Gaming GPUs, Professional Visualization).

2. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & REVENUE GROWTH
- Total Revenue for Fiscal Year 2024 grew by 126% YoY to $60.92 Billion.
- Data Center segment revenue reached a record $47.5 Billion, up 217% YoY, driven by massive demand for HGX H100 and Hopper architecture AI platforms.
- Gross Margin expanded significantly to 72.7%, reflecting strong pricing power and high-margin software stack (NVIDIA AI Enterprise).
- Net Income rose over 580% YoY to $29.76 Billion.

3. CORE COMPETITIVE STRENGTHS
- CUDA Ecosystem: Unrivaled developer software lock-in with over 4.5 million developers globally.
- Full-Stack Architecture: Proprietary hardware (H100, B200 Blackwell), Interconnects (NVLink, Quantum InfiniBand), and Software (NVIDIA AI Enterprise).
- Rapid Innovation Pace: Accelerated GPU release architecture cycle from 2 years down to 1 year.

4. KEY OPERATIONAL & REGULATORY RISKS
- Geopolitical & Export Restrictions: US Department of Commerce restrictions on high-performance AI chip sales to specific markets (China, Middle East).
- Supply Chain Concentration: Heavy dependence on TSMC for advanced 4nm/3nm wafer fabrication and CoWoS advanced packaging.
- Customer Concentration: Top cloud service providers (Hyperscalers like Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Google) account for over 40% of total revenue.

5. STRATEGIC OUTLOOK & FUTURE PLANS
- Launch of Blackwell Architecture (B200, GB200 NVL72) delivering 30x inference speedups over H100.
- Expansion into Sovereign AI initiatives with global telecommunications and nation-state data center infrastructure investments.
